2. Product goal

Create an application called:

English Mastery

The app should help a learner systematically improve English from beginner to advanced level.

The application must eventually support CEFR:

A1
A2
B1
B2
C1
C2

The core philosophy is:

Review old knowledge first, strengthen weak knowledge second, then learn new knowledge.

Do NOT implement a simple fixed rule such as "learn exactly 10 new words every day".

Instead, the application must generate an adaptive daily learning plan based on:

review items due today
weak vocabulary
weak grammar concepts
previous mistakes
user mastery
daily study target
maximum new vocabulary per day

Example user settings:

desiredRetention: 0.9

Example behavior:

If:

due reviews = 8

the learner may receive:

8 review
10 new words

If:

due reviews = 20

the learner may receive:

20 review
5 new words

If:

due reviews = 30

the learner should receive primarily reviews and possibly:
