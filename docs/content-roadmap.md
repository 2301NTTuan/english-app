# Content roadmap

Current baseline: a 6,000-unit source-audited vocabulary master inventory, of which 298 records have enriched lesson content; 138 grammar topics with 35 enriched records but zero lessons that meet the stricter production-detail rubric against a target of 110+; 30 idioms, 30 phrasal verbs, 40 collocations, and 7 common expressions.

1. Add provenance fields, editorial workflow, reviewer sign-off, and versioned release notes.
2. Review master vocabulary senses and progressively enrich high-frequency A1/A2 records with original bilingual content before advanced breadth.
3. Turn remaining grammar outlines into complete explanations, contrasts, practice, error analysis, and Vietnamese support.
4. Add multiple unambiguous templates per item and balance recall, production, listening, and context.
5. Reassess source-backed and editorial CEFR/frequency metadata against real learner evidence with consent/privacy review.
6. Add content deprecation redirects so published IDs never silently orphan history.

Every release requires schema/duplicate/reference validation, answer uniqueness, prerequisite-cycle checks, educator and translator review, accessibility review, provenance approval, and a migration rehearsal.
