# Content sources and licensing

The bundled vocabulary, grammar explanations, examples, mistakes, expressions, and placement items are repository-authored seed content. No third-party corpus attribution or redistribution license is currently recorded in machine-readable provenance fields. That absence is a launch blocker for material adapted from external sources.

Before publishing expanded content, record the source, canonical URL/publication, license, attribution, author/editor, review date, locale, and modification status per batch. Use only original, public-domain, or suitably licensed material. Preserve stable IDs and version/checksum releases. Require English educator, Vietnamese language, accessibility, and provenance review. Treat current CEFR/frequency metadata as editorial estimates unless calibrated against an approved source.

The current counts describe coverage, not corpus comprehensiveness or external certification.
