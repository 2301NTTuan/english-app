# Content sources and licensing

The bundled vocabulary, grammar explanations, examples, mistakes, expressions, placement passages, and placement items are repository-authored seed content. Placement batches now carry machine-readable provenance in `src/data/content-provenance.ts`; no third-party corpus text was imported. See `docs/content-quality.md` for the source-by-source licensing decisions and release policy.

Before publishing expanded content, record the source, canonical URL/publication, license, attribution, author/editor, review date, locale, and modification status per batch. Use only original, public-domain, or suitably licensed material. Preserve stable IDs and version/checksum releases. Require English educator, Vietnamese language, accessibility, and provenance review. Treat current CEFR/frequency/difficulty metadata as editorial estimates unless calibrated against an approved source.

The current counts describe coverage, not corpus comprehensiveness or external certification.
